What We Do
Virtual Employee specialises in cost effective & scalable offshore outsourcing and digital transformation services and solutions. We help businesses of all sizes from start up to enterprises with staff augmentation, project and process outsourcing and the establishment of captive units in India.
Our expansive portfolio of services includes specialized domains such as IT and ITES, software programming, legal services, product engineering, digital marketing, multimedia and animation and content creation. For non-technical functions, we provide comprehensive support in areas like but not limited to, customer support, back-office operations, virtual assistance, accounting, and transcription services.

Our Vision
“
To redefine the Future of Work through innovation, technology and A.I.”
While our journey began in 2007-2008 with a mission to revolutionize outsourcing, we now, strive to
disrupt the way we work.
The future of work has arrived:
The hardest resource to manage is Human Resources, a challenge that has become even more pronounced in a post-covid world.
At Virtual Employee we are developing technology and transitioning into an A.I. company to navigate and surmount the increasingly complex challenges in human resource management—overlooked by most companies because this isn't a core competency or a unique selling point for them. Drawing a parallel with cloud computing, we believe, particularly in a post-covid world that we can manage employees far more efficiently than our clients ever could in-house. In this vision, businesses would turn to us primarily for our technology and resource management, with cost savings as a valuable by-product.

What Do We Stand for?
Virtual Employee embodies the essence of entrepreneurial grit — the hard-won successes, the challenges, and the rollercoaster journey of business.
In Scotland,
James Flynn
alongside Surjeet from Virtual Employee, is revolutionizing food safety with cutting-edge software.
In Belgium,
Ludwig Vleugels
and his Virtual Employee counterpart are crafting technology poised to automate the intricacies of fibre management.
Meanwhile, in Chicago, a former police officer,
James Oliver
has joined forces with our embedded development team to create a life-saving product for emergency responders.
In an age where transactional relationships are becoming more common, we are dedicated to building a team united by a shared purpose. This commitment to a collective goal is at the core of what we stand for.
What Do We Not Stand for?
Are you looking for the best talent/solution locally, but the cheapest quote overseas? The pursuit of quality and the fixation on securing the 'cheapest quote' are often at odds. This race to the bottom, characterized by corner-cutting, is a compromise that does not align with our values. We stand firm in our dedication to delivering excellence without succumbing to the allure of cost-cutting shortcuts.
Quality Over Cost
What Are We Trying to Change?
Outsourcing encompasses far more than just 'sending specs of work overseas,' 'quotes,' and back-office functions.
We aim to transform the common perception and misconceptions about outsourcing, whether it be through innovation, research and development, team building, or fostering colleagues who become friends.
Why Did We Start?
Firstly,
we identified a distinct market gap, where VE perfectly positions itself as a middle ground between project outsourcing and freelancing. Our model bridges this gap, providing the cost benefits of offshoring while allowing clients full control over their projects.
Secondly,
our mission is to enable businesses, especially SMEs, to outsource in a manner akin to large multinational corporations (MNCs), thus democratizing access to talent and levelling the playing field in the global business operations. We were the pioneers of the VE offshoring model and remain its only provider offering the service in its most complete and genuine form.
